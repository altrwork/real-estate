# Close-out

1. Confirm closing and funding happened. Ask who gets what; confirm disbursement instructions with the user. Never state amounts or wire details that weren't supplied.
2. Draft thank-you / wrap notes to client, buyer side, attorneys, lender, title (drafts only).
3. Draft the "just closed" LinkedIn post from the flyer content (reuse `marketing-outreach`); avoid price unless the user okays it.
4. Update the CRM: stage closed, buyer/seller buy boxes learned, follow-up date.
